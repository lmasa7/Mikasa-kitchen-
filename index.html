Crispy Chicken Katsu Curry

Golden-brown panko-crusted chicken cutlet served alongside warm, savory Japanese curry sauce and steaming short-grain white rice.

Prep Time 25 mins
Cook Time 20 mins
Servings 3 Servings

Step-by-Step Masterclass

Ingredients Needed

  • 2 packs of fresh ramen noodles or ramen wheat flour
  • 500g cow neck bones and cow belly slices
  • 4 soft-boiled marinated eggs (Ajitsuke Tamago)
  • 2 stalks of fresh green onions, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic and a thumb-sized piece of ginger
  • 2 tablespoons of authentic Japanese soy sauce (Shoyu)
  • 1 tablespoon of toasted sesame oil and nori sheets

Cooking Instructions

  1. Blanch the cow bones in boiling water for 10 minutes to remove any impurities, then rinse thoroughly under cold water.
  2. In a large stockpot, combine the cleaned bones, sliced garlic, ginger, and scallions. Cover with water and simmer on medium-low for at least 10 hours until opaque and rich.
  3. Prepare the tare sauce by simmering soy sauce, mirin, and sake together until reduced by half. Set aside in a bowl.
  4. Boil the fresh ramen noodles in a separate pot of rapidly boiling water for exactly 90 seconds until al dente texture is reached.
  5. Assemble the bowls by pouring the hot rich broth over the tare sauce, add the drained noodles, and top neatly with sliced chashu, a halved marinated egg, and chopped green onions.
